Questions about ai-driven drug discovery

Can AI predictions demonstrate that a drug works?

No. Computational predictions can help prioritise hypotheses for further investigation. Experimental studies and subsequent development are needed to establish biological effects, safety and efficacy.

What can an early computational project deliver?

Depending on scope, it can deliver a documented screening workflow, target-related analysis or candidate prioritisation. The project should explain the data used, assumptions, evaluation approach and limits of the conclusions.
