Questions about bioinformatics & genomics

What information is needed to scope a bioinformatics project?

Share the research question, data type and approximate size, available metadata and intended outputs. Data access, quality checks, appropriate permissions and scientific review must be agreed before analysis.

What makes an analysis reproducible?

Documented inputs, software versions, parameters, processing steps and quality checks help another researcher understand and repeat the analysis. Deliverables should state which materials and execution instructions are included.

Can results be used for clinical decisions?

The services described here support research. Research analyses are not clinical diagnoses, and clinical use would require a separately established validation and governance pathway.
