Questions about websites & apps

What is included in a website development project?

The scope can include page design, responsive development, enquiry forms, agreed integrations and technical SEO foundations. We agree the page list, content responsibilities, revisions and handover before work begins.

Can you connect a website to our existing systems?

We can scope connections to systems with suitable APIs or supported exports. Share the tools you use and the information that needs to move between them so we can assess access, permissions and maintenance.

What affects the cost of a website or application?

The main factors are the number and complexity of pages or features, design requirements, integrations, content preparation and testing. A written scope separates initial development from hosting and ongoing support.
