Questions about vr / ar solutions

Should we choose VR or AR?

VR is suitable when the user needs a simulated environment. AR overlays information or objects on a view of the physical world. The decision depends on the learning or interaction goal and the devices available.

What should a prototype test?

A prototype should test the core interaction on the intended device: whether users understand the task, can navigate comfortably and can complete the intended action. Content quality and performance requirements are agreed in the scope.
