Questions about quantum computing

Can a quantum project begin without dedicated hardware?

Many exploratory projects can begin with circuit simulation. Simulator limits, hardware availability and the research question determine how far this approach can go.

Do you guarantee a quantum performance advantage?

No. Any claimed advantage needs a defined problem, appropriate classical comparisons and evidence. An exploratory project may find that a classical approach is more suitable.
