Questions about deep tech, ai & r&d

Can we commission a feasibility study before building a product?

Yes. A focused study can explore technical options, constraints and evidence needed before a development decision. The scope should define the question, available inputs and the decision the study will inform.

What does a proof of concept establish?

A proof of concept tests selected assumptions under agreed conditions. It is not automatically a production-ready product. The handover should distinguish demonstrated behaviour from unresolved engineering or research questions.
