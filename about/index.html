About HealOra

A studio built around doing the work properly.

HealOra is a software and AI studio based in Bolton, Greater Manchester. We design and build websites, business applications and AI systems — and take on research projects where deep technical work is needed.

Why we work the way we do

Most software projects don't fail on technology. They fail on vague scopes, silent assumptions and testing that never happens. So we built HealOra around the opposite: written scopes, open builds, honest testing and proper handovers.

We work with businesses, startups and research teams across the UK. Every project gets the same treatment, whether it's a marketing website or a research prototype — understand it properly, scope it in writing, build it in the open, test it like it matters.

What that means in practice

You talk to the person building it. No account managers, no handoffs, no game of telephone.

We write things down. Scopes, decisions and assumptions — documented as we go, handed over at the end.

We test before you do. Every workflow gets clicked through before launch, against the scope we agreed.
