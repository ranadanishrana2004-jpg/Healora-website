How we work

The process behind the work.

The same four steps on every project — whether it's a website or a research prototype.

01

First conversation

Tell us what needs to work better. We'll ask direct questions about users, constraints and budget — and say honestly if we're not the right fit.

02

Written scope

Deliverables, milestones and responsibilities, agreed in writing before a line of code is written.

03

Build in the open

You see working progress in stages you can click through. Decisions get documented, not buried in chat threads.

04

Test and hand over

We test against the agreed scope, fix what's off, then hand everything over with documentation and a walkthrough.
