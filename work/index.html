HOW WE WORK

A clear path from
idea to implementation.

Every project is different. A shared understanding of the problem, deliverables and review process gives the work a solid starting point.

01

Discover

Discuss the problem, users, available information and constraints. Define what success means for this project.

02

Define

Agree deliverables, responsibilities, milestones and the review process in a written scope.

03

Develop

Build or analyse in manageable stages, sharing progress and documenting significant decisions.

04

Review & hand over

Test the agreed deliverables, explain limitations and provide documentation. Agree support where needed.
